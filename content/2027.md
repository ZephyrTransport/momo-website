MoMo brings together the community working at the intersection of transportation modeling, data, and planning to connect, share ideas, and advance practice. We are excited to welcome practitioners, researchers, students, and innovators to Tempe next spring.

## theme: CONVERGE

The theme for this conference is _converge._

MoMo is the forum where ideas, tools, and perspectives converge to address complex mobility challenges. It is also the space where transportation professionals from public agencies, private consultants and academic institutes converge to shape the future of modeling mobility.

After all, as transportation modelers, our endgame is always convergence.

## date and place

April 12–15, 2027<br/>
Memorial Union, Arizona State University campus, Tempe, AZ

Dates for the call for abstracts, registration, etc. will be announced soon!
