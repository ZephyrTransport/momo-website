The Taste of MoMo webinar series highlights presentations and speakers from the previous Modeling Mobility Conference (MoMo). Taste of MoMo provides a free and accessible venue for professionals working at the intersection of transportation modeling, data, and planning to connect, share ideas, and advance practice. Webinars can be used for professional development and AICP credits.
